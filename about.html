The AfroBite Journey

Our Story

From the heart of Nigeria to the world's finest tables. Discover the heritage, passion, and artistry behind AfroBite Royale.

"AfroBite Royale was born from a simple conviction: Nigerian food deserves a place among the world's most celebrated cuisines. This is not just dinner. It is a homecoming, served on a royal stage."

Our Journey

Milestones of Excellence

2018

The Vision

AfroBite was founded in a small Lagos kitchen with a singular mission: to elevate Nigerian cuisine to fine-dining status without losing its soul.

2019

The First Flagship

We opened our doors in Victoria Island, Lagos. The response was overwhelming, confirming that the world was ready for luxury African dining.

2021

Expanding Horizons

Chef Amos Taiwo joined the team, bringing his Parisian training and deep-rooted Nigerian heritage to create our first signature tasting menu.

2023

Global Recognition

AfroBite Royale received international acclaim, including a recommendation from the Michelin Guide and a spot on Condé Nast's Hot List.

2025

The Royal Era

With locations in Lagos, Abuja, and London, we continue our mission to share the true taste of Africa with the world, one royal plate at a time.

The Visionary

Meet Chef Amos Taiwo

Executive Chef & Founder

Trained in Paris · Rooted in Lagos

Trained in the rigorous kitchens of Paris and inspired by the vibrant, chaotic beauty of Lagos, Chef Amos is the creative force behind AfroBite Royale.

His philosophy is simple: honour the ingredient, respect the tradition, and elevate the presentation to art. He believes that Nigerian food is not just sustenance; it is a story, a heritage, and a celebration.

Under his leadership, AfroBite Royale has become a beacon of culinary excellence, proving that African cuisine belongs on the world's most prestigious tables.
