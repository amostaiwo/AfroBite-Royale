Chef Amos Taiwo

Executive Chef · AfroBite Royale

Trained in Paris and Lagos, Chef Amos brings over two decades of culinary mastery to every plate. His philosophy is simple: honour the ingredient, respect the tradition, and elevate the presentation to art.

His signature dish, Egusi Elegance, reimagines a Nigerian classic with hand-pounded yam spheres, smoked fish pearls, and a delicate palm-oil emulsion — a dish that has earned him international acclaim.

Our Story

From the Heart of Nigeria to the World's Finest Tables

AfroBite Royale was born from a simple conviction: Nigerian food deserves a place among the world's most celebrated cuisines. We grew up around the aroma of bubbling egusi, the crackle of suya over open flame, and the joyous chaos of a Nigerian family kitchen.

Today, we translate those memories into a fine-dining experience that is unapologetically African and undeniably luxurious. Our chefs blend time-honoured recipes with contemporary technique, while our sommelier curates pairings that elevate every bite.

This is not just dinner. It is a homecoming, served on a royal stage.
